# Marionette 0.7.0

Marionette absorbs five of Charles Liu's design skills, from charles-skills, as five new
on-demand capabilities. `interface-polish` (charles-polish) takes an interface that
already works from fine to finished and checks its motion frame by frame. `color-critique`
(charles-color-analysis) measures the colours of a visual, a page, an app screen or a
palette and gives exact OKLCH and hex corrections with previews. `ux-laws`
(charles-design-laws) designs or reviews a screen or a flow by twenty laws of UX.
`design-tips` (charles-design-tips) applies or checks twenty practical tips on type,
spacing, colour, forms and copy. `interaction-motion` (charles-ux-animation) builds or
reviews, in the project's code, how an interface moves and responds. The last three
each have a build and a review intent. ui-ux-pro-max stays the lookup: where sources
give different numbers none is rewritten, and a system you recorded comes first. The
catalog goes from 38 to 43 capabilities and from 17 to 18 sources; nothing new acts in
every session. The upstream has no release or tag, so the pin follows its head (83de509d).

# Marionette 0.6.0

Marionette absorbs better-layout, Jakub Krehel's layout skill, as one new on-demand
capability, `layout-structure`: it sets or reviews the layout of an interface in code
(grouping and alignment, narrow and mobile viewports, safe areas, container queries,
RTL mirroring, room for translated text) and reports findings with a severity and a
verdict. The catalog goes from 37 to 38 capabilities and from 16 to 17 sources;
nothing new acts in every session. The upstream has no release or tag, so the pin
follows its head (672ae44a).

# Marionette 0.2.1

Marionette now gives brief ADHD progress updates while waiting for agents or long
operations, making important changes, and taking autonomous decisions. Updates
state what is done, what remains and the next step; autonomous decisions include
their reason. Existing ADHD mode controls and the rule against redundant closing
recaps remain in effect.

# Marionette 0.2.0

- New sources: open-paper-skills, TokenEconomizer, claude-design-system-prompt, Ponytail v5.3.3 and i-have-adhd v0.4.1. The catalog has 29 capabilities.
- Permanent policy: Ponytail and i-have-adhd in their own words, delivered natively on Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi and Hermes.
- Every capability was re-read against its pinned original. Rules that had been lost or softened are back; a body is an entry prompt that points to the packaged original, by file and section, for what a task needs.
- Nine source files the plugin does not need are no longer packaged.
- No limit of the package's own on what an absorbed command sends off the machine: the source's statement, the host's permissions and the user decide.
