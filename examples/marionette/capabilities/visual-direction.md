# Visual Direction (website and landing page)

Plans, deals and builds a company website or landing page the way the pinned taste-skill does: the kind of business decides the plan, the copy comes first, palette and type are dealt rather than chosen by taste, and the built page is looked at in screenshots before it is reported. Landing strategy from landing-page-design feeds the brief and the copy. `<TS>` below stands for `<package_root>/scripts/taste-skill` and `<LP>` for `<package_root>/scripts/ai-design-skills/skills/landing-page-design`.

## Behavior

The packaged originals are the method, in the sources' own words. Read them whole before working:

- `<TS>/SKILL.source.md`: the nine "House rules" and sections 1 to 6.
- `<TS>/references/copy.md`, `<TS>/references/system.md`, `<TS>/references/layout-v4.md` and `<TS>/references/sections.md`, which the source orders read "in full before the first page", and `<TS>/references/motion.md` ("Open the reference of every asset family the page uses first").
- For a landing page, `<LP>/SKILL.source.md`: "PART A — Strategy and structure" (A1 to A8), "Output format", "B8. Content realism", "B9. States", "B10. Ship requirements", and the "Strategy" and "Content and ship" blocks of "Quick checklist". Only these parts are the method here.

The steps below are an index into those files. Where this body and an original differ, the original wins, except for the changes listed under Sources, "Changes for coexistence (ADR-0017)". Landing strategy alone reads `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` and `<TS>/references/copy.md` only.

0. **Scope by the selected intent.** Landing strategy alone stops after step 2. A direction without a build stops after step 4. Record every output not produced as `not requested: <reason>`.
1. **Read the brief** (`<TS>/SKILL.source.md` section 1 and section 4 step 1). Write the kind on the plan's first line, from the table of section 1 (software, gaming, tech, product, service or place), then "what is sold, who buys it, the one main action (the transaction: Book a room, Order a cake, Start a free trial; a place with neither: Get directions) and the real material: logo, screens, photographs, prices, address, hours". For a landing page gather the intake of `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` A1 (Purpose, Audience and context, Proof and assets, Constraints): "Ask only for what is missing, and ask in one batch rather than one question at a time." "If the user cannot answer, make a reasonable assumption, state it in one line, and continue. Do not stall the build."
2. **Write all the copy first** (section 4 step 2) within the rules of `<TS>/references/copy.md`: "the h1 names the category and the buyer or the place (a game: its name and genre), one hard fact on the first screen, a noun in every heading, buttons as a verb and an object. Nothing is invented but a coined brand name (searched first, the result kept in `names.json` beside the page) and, on a place, plausible hours and prices, each marked `data-assumed`". For a landing page return, in order and before any code, the seven parts of `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` "Output format": the page outline, the hero copy ("headline, subheadline, CTA, proof line"), "three to five outcome driven bullets", "How it works — three steps", "FAQ — six to twelve questions and answers", "SEO / AEO — index or noindex recommendation, plus title and meta if indexed" and "Layout recommendation — A, B, C, or D, and why", under the conversion rules of A4 ("One primary CTA. Never place competing CTAs above the fold."; "Reduce risk. Pick at least one: free trial, free plan, no credit card, cancel anytime, money back guarantee."; "Move the FAQ earlier for high friction offers. Put proof directly beside the claim it supports.") and with the "Final CTA, identical to the top" (A2).
3. **Plan the page as screens** (section 4 step 3, with `<TS>/references/sections.md` and `<TS>/references/layout-v4.md` section 2): "Line 1: the kind. Line 2: the deal (step 4), with one sentence on why the palette fits the trade. Then one line per section: its id, its one idea, its picture and its kind (object, use, parts, world, slot, diagram, type; three object lines at most), its screens (1 or 2, from its content height, `layout-v4.md` section 2), which section holds a moment and why it moves (on tech also the effect or the scene)."
4. **Deal, never choose by taste** (section 4 step 4). "Run every command from the project folder":
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs deal --page <kind> --trade <trade> --history .taste-skill/history.json` ("add --axis after a word or editorial hero"). "Take the first palette and the first pairing printed (the next only when the first clashes with the client's own logo or photographs, or the pairing fails `hero-lines`, said on plan line 2)"; "never violet: deal again".
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs css --page <kind> --palette <id> --type <id> --radius soft --history .taste-skill/history.json`. "Radius: `sharp` for fintech and developer tools, `soft` by default, `round` for consumer apps, square on a place."
   - The client's own colours (a logo, a painted shopfront), "in place of css": `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs brand --page <kind> --bg "#.." --ink "#.." --accent "#.." [--surface "#.."] [--muted "#.."] --type <id> --radius soft`.
   - Nav, footer, close, rhythm, buttons and mark: the `node -e` one-liner of step 4 with the kind's lists exactly as that step prints them (a place: the line in `<TS>/references/sections.md` section 4, "The deal").
   - "Keep the `:root` block as printed." "Write every result on plan line 2 and in the comment at the top of `<body>`, and build exactly that".
   The hero and the moments are dealt in the source by `assets/layout/deal.mjs` and `scripts/moments.mjs`, which are not packaged. Choose the hero from `<TS>/references/layout-v4.md` section 3 ("The eleven heroes", "The place hero") and the moments from `<TS>/references/motion.md` for this brief, and say on plan line 2 that they were chosen, not dealt.
5. **Build** (section 4 steps 0 and 5). "An empty folder builds a static page"; "An existing project builds in its own stack"; "A brief that asks for Next.js builds a static export (`output: 'export'`)". The source builds from its kit (`assets/starter.html`, `system.css`, `system.js`, `assets/layout`, `assets/motion`, `assets/ui` and the other asset families), which is not packaged: do not copy or link it, and do not add the six stylesheet links that `start.mjs css` prints. Write the page's own markup and CSS to the classes, tokens and numbers that `<TS>/references/system.md`, `layout-v4.md`, `sections.md` and `motion.md` state, with the fonts link and the `:root` block as printed. Declare the kind on the page as section 1 says (`<html data-type>`, `<meta name="page-type">`, `<meta name="site-kind">`; a place adds `<meta name="page-kind" content="place">`). House rule 5 holds in full, including "Every hero enters once on load (`data-enter`), and every page carries one dealt moment, two on a long page"; the moments are the ones chosen in step 4. A built landing page also keeps `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` B8, B9 and B10.
6. **Check and look** (section 4 step 6, sections 5 and 6). Of the source's check commands only `shoot.mjs` is packaged. The source's rule for it holds: "Only the skill's scripts, each running one headless Chrome with WebGL on the CPU, closed by its own PID, one at a time in the foreground with a 10-minute timeout; never in the background, a windowed browser or a browser tool." Run `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/shoot.mjs --url <page> --w 1440 --out shots/1440.png --profile .taste-skill/chrome-check`, then "Shoot 1024, 820x1180, 768x1024 and 390 as well" (`--w 820 --h 1180`, `--w 768 --h 1024`). "read the copy aloud", then "shoot again and read every screenshot against the checklist": section 5 line by line (the ids in brackets and braces belong to checkers that are not packaged, so each line is judged from the screenshots and the page's code) and the "Never" list of section 6. Fix and shoot again, "three rounds at most".
7. **Record** for the handoff below, and report what section 4 step 7 lists as far as it exists here (the kind, the deal and why the palette fits, what you could not check, every assumed fact and the coined name's search, the photographs and art the client must supply):
   - `offer`, `page_outline`: the strategy and outline (for a non-landing website, the main action and the screen plan).
   - `palette`, `typography`: the dealt or brand values and ids.
   - `layout`: the hero, the sections by screen and the moments.
   - `constraints`: the rules later steps must keep, including the motion budget, and any deviation from the source with its reason.
   - `page`: the files written and the screenshots, or `not requested`.

## Specifics

- **House rules (pinned taste-skill):** the nine house rules of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` ("These nine win over everything else in this skill") and its section 6 "Never" hold as written there. They are not restated here.
- **The user's explicit instruction.** landing-page-design: "When the user's explicit prompt conflicts with a rule, the user wins." In this step that sentence holds for both sources: follow the instruction and record the deviation and its reason in `constraints`. A client's brand is not by itself such an instruction: "the client's own colours go through `start.mjs brand`", "Real logos keep their own full colours", and for the page's own design `<TS>/references/system.md` ("Colour") says "Never violet or lavender: a token whose OKLCH hue falls between about 270 and 330 degrees is turned to 255 (blue) or 345 (rose), whichever is nearer".
- **One owner of the look and of the page.** This step owns palette, typography and layout, and taste-skill is their method (`<TS>/references/system.md`: "never add a second grid, spacing scale, type scale, radius, button, card, hairline, fade or link style"). landing-page-design's "PART B — Visual system" B1 to B7 and B11 are not applied: they would be a second visual system. Where its Part A and taste-skill give different answers for a built page (section order, headline and button wording, proof rows, missing facts), taste-skill's plan in `sections.md` and its rules in `copy.md` decide; the landing outline supplies the offer, the argument and the copy that go into them. Later steps consume these outputs and do not restyle independently.
- **Not packaged from the source:** the hero and moment dealers (`deal.mjs`, `moments.mjs`), the kit (`assets/` stylesheets, layouts, UI fragments, icons), the photograph and icon tools (`photos.mjs`, `icons.mjs`), and the automated checks (`copy-lint.mjs`, `consistency.mjs`, `check.mjs`, `perf.mjs`, `gate.mjs`). Do not call them and do not claim their results. Where a step of the original needs one of them, say in the report that it was not run.
- `start.mjs deal` writes `.taste-skill/` in the project (history and the finish-gate marker); tell the user. Nothing is written outside the project unless `TASTE_SKILL_RECENT` is set.

## Checks

- **single_primary_action**: one offer or main action is explicit, with no competing primary action on the first screen.
- **proof_supported**: every claim, price, figure and testimonial comes from the user's material or is left out; the only assumptions are the two that `<TS>/references/copy.md` allows ("What may be assumed"), each marked `data-assumed` and listed in the report.
- **palette_applied**: the page uses the dealt or brand `:root` block as printed; the plan states the palette and why it fits the trade.
- **typography_consistent**: one heading scale from the dealt pairing; its faces are loaded.
- **house_rules_met**: the nine house rules and the "Never" list of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` hold on the page, or each deviation is recorded in `constraints` with the user's explicit instruction.
- **screens_reviewed**: screenshots at 1440, 1024, 820x1180, 768x1024 and 390 were taken with shoot.mjs and read against the checklist; only a browser that could not be installed makes this not-tested.
- **automated_gate_disclosed**: the report says that the source's automated checker and finish gate are not packaged and were not run.
