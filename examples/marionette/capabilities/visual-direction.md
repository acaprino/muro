# Visual Direction (website and landing page)

Plans, deals, builds and checks a company website or landing page the way the pinned taste-skill does: the kind of business decides the plan, the copy comes first, the hero, palette, type and moments are dealt rather than chosen by taste, the page is built from the skill's kit, and the skill's checker and finish gate run in a real browser before it is reported. Landing strategy from landing-page-design feeds the brief and the copy. `<TS>` below stands for `<package_root>/scripts/taste-skill`, the source's `<skill>` folder, packaged whole (`assets/`, `library/`, `references/`, `scripts/`), and `<LP>` for `<package_root>/scripts/ai-design-skills/skills/landing-page-design`.

## Behavior

The packaged originals are the full version of this method: `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` with its twenty-two references, and `<LP>/SKILL.source.md`. Open the part a step names when the task reaches it. Where this body and an original differ, the original wins, except for the changes listed under Sources. Where a script's message or a reference says `SKILL.md`, read `<TS>/SKILL.source.md`; where it says `<skill>`, read `<TS>`.

0. **Scope by the selected intent.** Landing strategy alone stops after step 2, arms no gate and opens only `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` and the copy references of step 2. A direction without a build stops after step 4: the deal has armed the finish gate in the project folder, so close it with `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/gate.mjs --done "<the user's words asking for the direction only>"`. Record every output not produced as `not requested: <reason>`.
1. **Read the brief and arm the gate.** Write the kind on the plan's first line (software, gaming, tech, product, service or place) from the table in `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "1. The kind of business decides everything", which also gives the page type and site-kind declared in step 5. Then what is sold, who buys it, the one main action (the transaction: Book a room, Order a cake, Start a free trial; a place with neither: Get directions) and the real material: logo, screens, photographs, prices, address, hours. Then arm the finish gate, before any page exists, from the project folder: `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/gate.mjs --start index.html --kind <kind> --category "<what is sold>" --primary "<main action>"`. A framework build names its dev server's URL or its built page in place of `index.html` and rebuilds before every gate run ("4. The run" step 1). For a landing page gather `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` "A1. Intake": ask only for what is missing, in one batch; if the user cannot answer, assume reasonably, state it in one line and continue.
2. **Write all the copy first,** with the copy layer of `<TS>/references/copy-voice.md` (the fact sheet, the voice card on the plan, the page's shape, the device budget; a register from `copy-registers.md`, the moves of `copy-moves.md`) within the rules of `<TS>/references/copy.md`. The h1 names the category and the buyer or the place (a game: its name and genre), the first screen holds one hard fact, every heading has a noun, buttons are a verb and an object. Nothing is invented but a coined brand name (searched first, the result kept in `names.json` beside the page) and, on a place, plausible hours and prices, each marked `data-assumed` (`copy.md`, "What may be assumed"). A draft kept in a `.md` file lints the same way as the page.
   For a landing page return, in order and before any code, the seven parts of `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` "Output format", with the counts it gives: page outline, hero copy, benefits, how it works, FAQ, SEO/AEO (index or noindex by "A7. SEO and AEO") and the layout recommendation (by "A3. Layout selection"). From "A4. Conversion rules": one primary CTA, never a competing CTA above the fold; at least one risk reducer (free trial, free plan, no credit card, cancel anytime, money back guarantee); Move the FAQ earlier for high friction offers. Put proof directly beside the claim it supports. The final CTA is identical to the top one. Open A2, A4, A5 and A8 for the section list, the remaining conversion rules, the copy formulas and the pitfalls.
3. **Plan the page as screens.** Line 1: the kind. Line 2: the deal of step 4, with one sentence on why the palette fits the trade. Then one line per section: its id, its one idea, its picture and the picture's kind (object, use, parts, world, slot, diagram, type; three object lines at most), its screens (1 or 2, from its content height: `<TS>/references/layout-v4.md` section 2), which section holds a moment and why it moves (on tech also the effect or the scene). Pick the photographs now (`<TS>/references/photos.md`); their count, stand-ins included, sets a place's length. Then read `cards.md` and the recipe of every id you use in `sections-library.md`. The plan comes from `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "2. The page plan by kind" and `<TS>/references/sections.md`; "3. What serves which page" names the asset family and the reference for each need: open the reference of every asset family the page uses first.
4. **Deal, never choose by taste.** Run every command from the project folder; each keeps a history, so pages differ:
   - The hero: `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/assets/layout/deal.mjs --type <page type> --has <photo,ui,film,object,setting> --history .taste-skill/layout-history.json` (a game adds `--kind gaming`, hardware `--kind tech`). A place's hero is printed, never dealt (`--type place`).
   - Palette and pairing: `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs deal --page <kind> --trade <trade> --history .taste-skill/history.json` (add `--axis` after a word or editorial hero), then `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs css --page <kind> --palette <id> --type <id> --radius soft --history .taste-skill/history.json`. The client's own colours (a logo, a painted shopfront) go through `brand` in place of `css`: `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs brand --page <kind> --bg "#.." --ink "#.." --accent "#.." [--surface "#.."] [--muted "#.."] --type <id> --radius soft`.
   - The moments, every kind: `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/moments.mjs pick --page <kind> --count 1|2 --fits section,statement --photo --history .taste-skill/history.json`.
   - Nav, footer, close, rhythm, buttons and mark: the `node -e` one-liner of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "4. The run" step 4 with the kind's lists exactly as printed there (a place: the whole line in `<TS>/references/sections.md` section 4, "The deal", read in its order).
   - Take the first palette and the first pairing printed; the next only when the first clashes with the client's own logo or photographs, or the pairing fails `hero-lines`, said on plan line 2. Never violet: deal again. A dealt part that needs a photograph the page lacks is dealt again. Keep the `:root` block as printed. Radius: `sharp` for fintech and developer tools, `soft` by default, `round` for consumer apps, square on a place. The `--has` rules for a product without photographs, a render and a game, and the tech-only lit ground, CSS grounds and one effect: the same step 4.
   - Write every result on plan line 2 and in the comment at the top of `<body>`, and build exactly that.
5. **Build from the kit.** An empty folder builds a static page: copy `<TS>/assets` into `assets` and start from `assets/starter.html`. An existing project builds in its own stack, its components taking the starter's markup and classes, with the kit copied into its public folder (`public/assets`) and loaded from there as plain files. A brief that asks for Next.js builds a static export (`output: 'export'`), checked on its built page (`--root out`). Before the first page read `<TS>/references/copy.md`, `copy-voice.md`, `system.md`, `layout-v4.md` and `sections.md` in full, as the source orders. Then build as `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "4. The run" step 5 says, line by line: the `<html>` attributes, the three metas and the head block in `starter.html`; only the `<section class="hero …" data-enter>` of `assets/layout/hero-<id>.html`; the dealt close and footer moved out of their `<template>`s; every section `main > section` with one `.wrap`, built to its recipe with the system's classes and tokens, page CSS only where a reference says page CSS; UI pasted from `assets/ui/<name>/fragment.html` with every name, number and logo rewritten to the client's; `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/icons.mjs scan index.html --out assets/icons --colour` last; an effect, object, moment or scene mounted exactly as its reference says, never from your own script; a moment's section copied from `assets/moments/_demo/in-page.html` or `in-page-service.html`; the form from the starter; the one footer credits line from `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/photos.mjs credits`; the hero's tile from `shoot.mjs` beside the page as `share.jpg`. A built landing page also keeps `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` "B8. Content realism", "B9. States" and "B10. Ship requirements", within the changes under Sources.
6. **Check and look.** Only the skill's scripts, each running one headless Chrome, one at a time in the foreground with a 10-minute timeout; never in the background, a windowed browser or a browser tool. Each serves the page itself (`--root <build folder>` for a built page whose links start at the site root); `--profile` is a folder in the OS temp folder or the project's `.taste-skill` (`.taste-skill/chrome-check`). From the project folder:
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/copy-lint.mjs index.html --kind <kind>`
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/consistency.mjs index.html --widths 1440,1024,820,768,390 --rhythm-widths 1440,820,768,390 --profile <dir> --page <kind> --category "<what is sold>" --primary "<main action>"`
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/assets/layout/check.mjs --url index.html --profile <dir> --sizes 1440x900,1024x768,820x1180,768x1024,390x844 --shots shots/layout`
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/shoot.mjs --url index.html --w 1440 --out shots/1440.png --profile <dir>`, then 1024, 820x1180 (`--w 820 --h 1180`), 768x1024 and 390
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/perf.mjs index.html --profile <dir>`

   Every `check.mjs` finding fails the page (no `--no-stress`). Fix every FAIL (a copy-lint WARN too, or say why it stays), read the copy aloud (`copy-voice.md` section 6), shoot again and read every screenshot against `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "5. Checklist" line by line and "6. Never". In the fix rounds run `consistency.mjs --quick --widths 1440,390`. Then the gate: `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/gate.mjs index.html`. Fix what it lists and run it again until its last line is `GATE: PASS` or `GATE: OPEN`, three rounds at most. When the user tells you to stop, or to ship the page as it is, run `gate.mjs --done "<what they said>"` (`GATE: DONE`) and report the open FAILs. `GATE: SETUP`: arm what a browser can open (step 1) and run it again. `GATE: ERROR` or `GATE: OFF`: put the line in the report and finish; do not rerun. Never edit the kit's files or hide anything from the checker. A FAIL you judge false is waived in `.taste-skill/waivers.json` with its reason, never fixed by gaming the check (`<TS>/references/gate.md`).
7. **Report and record.** Report what "4. The run" step 7 lists, in its order: the `GATE:` line first, then every waiver with its reason and every open FAIL; the kind and the deal; the voice card and every copy-lint WARN kept; the checker, layout and perf results; what you could not check; every assumed fact and the coined name's search; the form's `.example` stand-in and the canonical domain still to come; the photographs and art the client must supply, every stand-in frame among them; every client slot. Then record for the handoff below:
   - `offer`, `page_outline`: the strategy and outline (for a non-landing website, the main action and the screen plan).
   - `palette`, `typography`: the dealt or brand values and ids.
   - `layout`: the dealt hero, the sections by screen and the dealt moments.
   - `constraints`: the rules later steps must keep, including the motion budget, the gate's last line with its waivers and open FAILs, and any deviation from the source with its reason.
   - `page`: the files written and the screenshots, or `not requested`.

## Specifics

- **House rules.** Read the nine "House rules" and "6. Never" of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` before planning a page. The nine win over everything else in that skill.
- **The user's explicit instruction wins** over a rule of either source: follow it and record the deviation and its reason in `constraints`. A client's brand is not by itself such an instruction: its colours go through `start.mjs brand`, real logos keep their own colours, and in the page's own design a violet or lavender token is still turned to blue or rose (`<TS>/references/system.md` "Colour").
- **A project design file is such an instruction.** A `DESIGN.md` of the project that is not a starter or a reference (the recorded `design_file`, or the file found in the project) counts as the user's explicit instruction for the values it confirms: its confirmed colour tokens are the page's colours as the file has them, set on the page's `--p-*` variables by hand and not through `start.mjs brand`, which derives surface, muted and inverse colours the file does not hold; a violet token stays as recorded. Its typefaces, spacing, radius and component values are followed where a rule of either source says otherwise. Each deviation is recorded in `constraints`. Its `unresolved` entries and `proposed` values are not instructions, and where the file is silent the method decides. This step never rewrites `DESIGN.md`: a value the page needs and the file lacks is asked of the user, and the change belongs to `design-file` in its own step. A request in the turn that contradicts the file is followed and said to do so. Where the project runs design code checks (`design-check.config.json`), source code in the checked paths with a colour value, a colour function such as `rgb()` or `oklch()`, a utility palette class, a font family or a `must-avoid` effect the file does not permit fails them: use the project's tokens there, or say that the check will fail.
- **One owner of the look and of the page.** This step owns palette, typography and layout by taste-skill's method, which allows no second grid, scale, radius, button, card or link style; landing-page-design's "PART B — Visual system" B1 to B7 and B11 are not applied. Where its Part A and taste-skill answer differently for a built page (section order, headline and button wording, proof rows, missing facts), taste-skill's `sections.md` and `copy.md` decide; the landing outline supplies the offer, the argument and the copy. Later steps consume these outputs and do not restyle independently.
- **The finish gate also runs when the agent stops.** On Claude Code and Codex the release registers `gate.mjs --hook` on the host's Stop event, as `<TS>/references/gate.md` "As a hook" describes: with no build armed in the folder it exits at once; with one armed it runs the checks and blocks the stop while fixable FAILs remain, within the rounds, and never traps the agent (its limits are in that section and at the top of `gate.mjs`). To ask the user something after a block, just stop again. On the other hosts no hook is registered: the gate is the run by hand of step 6. A subagent is not held by the hook; run the manual gate inside it.
- **Photographs and credits.** The library under `<TS>/assets/photos` and the pictures in the kit's demo folders are third parties' photographs under the licence each entry of `photos.json` or `credits.json` names (Unsplash License, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, Public Domain Mark). Print their credits in the page's one footer line (`photos.mjs credits`) and write "adapted" after a photograph shown through an effect or a colour grade, as those files order. A stand-in is listed in the report as one, never passed off as the client's.
- **What the tools fetch and write.** `photos.mjs search` asks Wikimedia Commons and the photograph tool downloads frames from picsum.photos and upload.wikimedia.org; `icons.mjs fetch` and `scan` download icon files from cdn.jsdelivr.net and GitHub into the project; the fonts link `start.mjs css` prints loads from Google Fonts when the page is opened. The checkers serve the page on 127.0.0.1 only. The run writes `assets/`, `shots/` and `.taste-skill/` (deal histories, the build marker, `gate.json`, waivers, Chrome profiles) in the project; tell the user. Nothing is written outside the project unless `TASTE_SKILL_RECENT` is set.
- **The package folder is read-only.** Run every script from the project folder and never write into `<TS>`. `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/assets/scenes/_demo/shoot.mjs index` is run only with `--out assets/scenes` (the project's own index): see the change under Sources.

## Checks

- **single_primary_action**: one offer or main action is explicit, with no competing primary action on the first screen.
- **proof_supported**: every claim, price, figure and testimonial comes from the user's material or is left out; the only assumptions are the two that `<TS>/references/copy.md` "What may be assumed" allows, each marked `data-assumed` and listed in the report.
- **palette_applied**: the page uses the dealt or brand `:root` block as printed, or the confirmed values of a project design file with each deviation recorded in `constraints`; the plan states the palette and why it fits the trade.
- **typography_consistent**: one heading scale from the dealt pairing, or from the confirmed typography of a project design file; its faces are loaded.
- **house_rules_met**: the nine house rules and the "Never" list of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` hold on the page, or each deviation is recorded in `constraints` with the user's explicit instruction.
- **screens_reviewed**: screenshots at 1440, 1024, 820x1180, 768x1024 and 390 were taken with shoot.mjs and read against the checklist; only a browser that could not be installed makes this not-tested.
- **checks_run**: `copy-lint.mjs`, `consistency.mjs` at the five widths, `assets/layout/check.mjs` at the five sizes and `perf.mjs` ran on the final page. The evidence gives each one's FAIL count and names every FAIL left, with its waiver or as open. A script that could not run makes this not-tested, with its error.
- **finish_gate_closed**: the gate was armed before the page existed, and the evidence quotes the last line of its last run by hand. `pass` only for `GATE: PASS`. `GATE: OPEN` and `GATE: DONE` are `fail`, with the open FAILs and the waivers listed (for `DONE`, the user's words). `GATE: ERROR`, `GATE: OFF` and `GATE: SETUP` are `not-tested`, with the line.
