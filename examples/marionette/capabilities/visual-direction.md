# Visual Direction (website and landing page)

Plans, deals and builds a company website or landing page the way the pinned taste-skill does: the kind of business decides the plan, the copy comes first, palette and type are dealt rather than chosen by taste, and the built page is looked at in screenshots before it is reported. Landing strategy from landing-page-design feeds the brief and the copy. `<TS>` below stands for `<package_root>/scripts/taste-skill` and `<LP>` for `<package_root>/scripts/ai-design-skills/skills/landing-page-design`.

## Behavior

The packaged originals are the full version of this method: `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` with its five packaged references, and `<LP>/SKILL.source.md`. Open the part a step names when the task reaches it. Where this body and an original differ, the original wins, except for the changes listed under Sources.

0. **Scope by the selected intent.** Landing strategy alone stops after step 2 and opens only `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` and `<TS>/references/copy.md`. A direction without a build stops after step 4. Record every output not produced as `not requested: <reason>`.
1. **Read the brief.** Write the kind on the plan's first line (software, gaming, tech, product, service or place) from the table in `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "1. The kind of business decides everything", which also gives the page type and site-kind declared in step 5. Then what is sold, who buys it, the one main action (the transaction: Book a room, Order a cake, Start a free trial; a place with neither: Get directions) and the real material: logo, screens, photographs, prices, address, hours. For a landing page gather `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` "A1. Intake": ask only for what is missing, in one batch; if the user cannot answer, assume reasonably, state it in one line and continue.
2. **Write all the copy first,** inside `<TS>/references/copy.md` (open "Five rules", "Headlines and text" and "Banned" before writing; "What may be assumed" and "Contacts the brief does not give" when a fact is missing). The h1 names the category and the buyer or the place (a game: its name and genre), the first screen holds one hard fact, every heading has a noun, buttons are a verb and an object. Nothing is invented but a coined brand name (searched first, the result kept in `names.json` beside the page) and, on a place, plausible hours and prices, each marked `data-assumed`.
   For a landing page return, in order and before any code, the seven parts of `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` "Output format", with the counts it gives: page outline, hero copy, benefits, how it works, FAQ, SEO/AEO (index or noindex by "A7. SEO and AEO") and the layout recommendation (by "A3. Layout selection"). From "A4. Conversion rules": one primary CTA, never a competing CTA above the fold; at least one risk reducer (free trial, free plan, no credit card, cancel anytime, money back guarantee); Move the FAQ earlier for high friction offers. Put proof directly beside the claim it supports. The final CTA is identical to the top one. Open A2, A4, A5 and A8 for the section list, the remaining conversion rules, the copy formulas and the pitfalls.
3. **Plan the page as screens.** Line 1: the kind. Line 2: the deal of step 4, with one sentence on why the palette fits the trade. Then one line per section: its id, its one idea, its picture and the picture's kind (object, use, parts, world, slot, diagram, type; three object lines at most), its screens (1 or 2, from its content height: `<TS>/references/layout-v4.md` "2. One section per screen"), which section holds a moment and why it moves (on tech also the effect or the scene). The plan comes from `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "2. The page plan by kind" and the kind's part of `<TS>/references/sections.md` ("1. Plan in screens", "3. The plans"; a place: "4. Place").
4. **Deal, never choose by taste.** Run every command from the project folder:
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs deal --page <kind> --trade <trade> --history .taste-skill/history.json` (add `--axis` after a word or editorial hero). Take the first palette and the first pairing printed; the next only when the first clashes with the client's own logo or photographs, or the pairing fails `hero-lines`, and say so on plan line 2. Never violet: deal again.
   - `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs css --page <kind> --palette <id> --type <id> --radius soft --history .taste-skill/history.json`. Radius: `sharp` for fintech and developer tools, `soft` by default, `round` for consumer apps, square on a place.
   - The client's own colours (a logo, a painted shopfront) go through `brand` in place of `css`: `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/start.mjs brand --page <kind> --bg "#.." --ink "#.." --accent "#.." [--surface "#.."] [--muted "#.."] --type <id> --radius soft`.
   - Nav, footer, close, rhythm, buttons and mark: run the `node -e` one-liner of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "4. The run" step 4 with the kind's lists exactly as printed there (a place: the whole line in `<TS>/references/sections.md` "4. Place", "The deal", read in its order).
   - The hero and the moments are chosen, not dealt (their dealers are not packaged): the hero from `<TS>/references/layout-v4.md` "3. The hero" ("The eleven heroes", "The place hero"), the moments from `<TS>/references/motion.md` "The rule: every page moves, in three places". Say on plan line 2 that they were chosen.
   - Keep the `:root` block as printed. Write every result on plan line 2 and in the comment at the top of `<body>`, and build exactly that.
5. **Build.** An empty folder builds a static page, an existing project builds in its own stack, a brief that asks for Next.js builds a static export (`output: 'export'`). Before the first page read `<TS>/references/copy.md`, `system.md`, `layout-v4.md` and `sections.md` in full, as the source orders, and `<TS>/references/motion.md` for the motion the page uses. The source's kit (`assets/`) is not packaged: do not copy or link it, and do not add the six stylesheet links that `start.mjs css` prints. Write the page's own markup and CSS to the classes, tokens and numbers those references state, with the fonts link and the `:root` block as printed. Declare the kind as section 1 says (`<html data-type>`, `<meta name="page-type">`, `<meta name="site-kind">`; a place adds `<meta name="page-kind" content="place">`); "4. The run" step 5 has the other `<html>` attributes, the head block and the section structure. Every hero enters once on load (`data-enter`) and every page carries one moment, two on a long page: those chosen in step 4. A built landing page also keeps `<LP>/SKILL.source.md` "B8. Content realism", "B9. States" and "B10. Ship requirements", within the changes under Sources.
6. **Check and look.** Of the source's check scripts only `shoot.mjs` is packaged. It runs one headless Chrome and serves the page itself (`--root <build folder>` for a built page whose links start at the site root). Run it one at a time, in the foreground, with a 10-minute timeout; never in the background, and never a windowed browser or a browser tool in its place. `node <package_root>/scripts/taste-skill/scripts/shoot.mjs --url <page> --w 1440 --out shots/1440.png --profile .taste-skill/chrome-check`, then 1024, 820x1180 (`--w 820 --h 1180`), 768x1024 and 390. Read the copy aloud, then read every screenshot against `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` "5. Checklist" line by line (its bracketed ids belong to checkers that are not packaged: judge each line from the screenshots and the code) and "6. Never". Fix and shoot again, three rounds at most.
7. **Record** for the handoff below, and report what "4. The run" step 7 lists as far as it exists here: the kind, the deal and why the palette fits, what you could not check, every assumed fact and the coined name's search, the photographs and art the client must supply.
   - `offer`, `page_outline`: the strategy and outline (for a non-landing website, the main action and the screen plan).
   - `palette`, `typography`: the dealt or brand values and ids.
   - `layout`: the hero, the sections by screen and the moments.
   - `constraints`: the rules later steps must keep, including the motion budget, and any deviation from the source with its reason.
   - `page`: the files written and the screenshots, or `not requested`.

## Specifics

- **House rules.** Read the nine "House rules" and "6. Never" of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` before planning a page. The nine win over everything else in that skill.
- **The user's explicit instruction wins** over a rule of either source: follow it and record the deviation and its reason in `constraints`. A client's brand is not by itself such an instruction: its colours go through `start.mjs brand`, real logos keep their own colours, and in the page's own design a violet or lavender token is still turned to blue or rose (`<TS>/references/system.md` "Colour").
- **One owner of the look and of the page.** This step owns palette, typography and layout by taste-skill's method, which allows no second grid, scale, radius, button, card or link style; landing-page-design's "PART B — Visual system" B1 to B7 and B11 are not applied. Where its Part A and taste-skill answer differently for a built page (section order, headline and button wording, proof rows, missing facts), taste-skill's `sections.md` and `copy.md` decide; the landing outline supplies the offer, the argument and the copy. Later steps consume these outputs and do not restyle independently.
- **Not packaged from the source:** the hero and moment dealers (`deal.mjs`, `moments.mjs`), the kit (`assets/` stylesheets, layouts, UI fragments, icons), the photograph and icon tools (`photos.mjs`, `icons.mjs`), the automated checks (`copy-lint.mjs`, `consistency.mjs`, `check.mjs`, `perf.mjs`, `gate.mjs`) and every reference file other than the five named in the steps. Do not call them and do not claim their results; where the original needs one, say in the report that it was not run.
- `start.mjs deal` writes `.taste-skill/` in the project (history and the finish-gate marker); tell the user. Nothing is written outside the project unless `TASTE_SKILL_RECENT` is set.

## Checks

- **single_primary_action**: one offer or main action is explicit, with no competing primary action on the first screen.
- **proof_supported**: every claim, price, figure and testimonial comes from the user's material or is left out; the only assumptions are the two that `<TS>/references/copy.md` "What may be assumed" allows, each marked `data-assumed` and listed in the report.
- **palette_applied**: the page uses the dealt or brand `:root` block as printed; the plan states the palette and why it fits the trade.
- **typography_consistent**: one heading scale from the dealt pairing; its faces are loaded.
- **house_rules_met**: the nine house rules and the "Never" list of `<TS>/SKILL.source.md` hold on the page, or each deviation is recorded in `constraints` with the user's explicit instruction.
- **screens_reviewed**: screenshots at 1440, 1024, 820x1180, 768x1024 and 390 were taken with shoot.mjs and read against the checklist; only a browser that could not be installed makes this not-tested.
- **automated_gate_disclosed**: the report says that the source's automated checker and finish gate are not packaged and were not run.
