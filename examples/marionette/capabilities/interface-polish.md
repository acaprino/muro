# Interface Polish (score, fix, verify frame by frame)

Pushes an interface that works until it feels finished, or reviews it against the same bar. Most "something feels off" problems are one wrong note: look, measure and name it before changing anything. `<PL>` below stands for `<package_root>/scripts/charles-skills/skills/design/charles-polish`. The method is `<PL>/SKILL.source.md`; the capture and measuring scripts are in `<PL>/scripts`. Open a section of the original when a step names it. The original is the full version and wins where this body differs, except for the changes listed in the Sources section.

## Behavior

1. **Render it** at its real size and at the smallest size it will be seen, and capture each state and each motion as frames (step 7 has the scripts). Measure in the pixels people see: on a scaled stage a 42 px button may be 37 px on screen.
2. **Score it** against the anchors of `## Run it`, `### Score anchors` (3 works and looks like everything else; 5 designed at rest, but unhappy states or motion are default; 7 every state is designed and motion is correct, nothing is memorable yet; 8 one thing people would screenshot and no visible defect at the viewing size; 9 that thing holds up in every state and input path, and a rival would copy it; 10 is never awarded to your own work) and name the weakest thing on screen in one sentence.
3. **Structure**: fix hierarchy and containers before details. Polishing the wrong structure is wasted work.
4. **States**: map every state, including empty, loading, error and success, and design the unhappy ones.
5. **Details**: run every row of the table in `## Details`, in the project's own styling system. Short on time, start with form behaviour, hit area, contrast and layout shift. Among its rows: `tabular-nums` on dynamic numbers; never change `font-weight` on hover or selection; fixed dimensions or `aspect-ratio` so nothing shifts; at most 5 type sizes; body and labels ≥4.5:1 and large text and icons ≥3:1 over the real background, nothing people must read under 12 px at the viewing size; press at `scale(0.96)`, never below 0.95; hover on named properties at about 150ms, gated to `(hover: hover)`, with no layout change; never remove focus, a 2px ring at 3:1 or more; hit areas ≥44×44pt on iOS, ≥48×48dp on Android and ≥40×40px on desktop, through a pseudo-element and never overlapping; 16px inputs with real labels.
6. **Motion**: walk the three decision trees of `## Motion` for every animation (`### Should it animate?`: 100+ times a day or fired from a keyboard shortcut means no animation, occasional means under 300ms, rare or once may be choreographed up to about 2.5s, and motion that answers no question is cut; `### Which curve?`; `### How long?`: exits run 20–30% faster and quieter than enters), then its `### Rules` (animate `transform` and `opacity`; interruptible by default; enter at `scale(0.95)` and `opacity: 0`, never `scale(0)`; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: same information, no movement) and, for multi-stage or physical motion, `### Physical motion and choreography` with a storyboard of named timing constants. `### Sound and haptics` applies only to rare or physical moments.
7. **Verify frame by frame** (`## Verify frame by frame`). Motion that was not looked at frame by frame is not verified. Install the scripts once with `npm install --prefix <PL>/scripts`, then:
   - capture on a virtual clock: `node <PL>/scripts/web-frames.mjs <url or file.html> --motion 1200 --trigger click:<selector> --widths 390,1440` (triggers `load`, `click:`, `hover:`, `scroll:<y>`, `js:<code>`; `--clip`, `--reduced-motion`, `--scheme dark`; without `--motion` it captures stills at every width). Pass `--out` with a scratch folder. Every run writes `console.txt`, which must be empty;
   - plot before you read: `node <PL>/scripts/frame-diff.mjs <frames folder>` marks pops as spikes and dead stretches in grey; read the frames it names first, then the whole contact sheet;
   - recordings: `<PL>/scripts/sim.sh start out.mp4`, the interaction, `sim.sh stop out.mp4`, then `node <PL>/scripts/sheet.mjs out.mp4 sheet.png --auto --keep frames/` and `frame-diff.mjs frames/`;
   - measure, do not eyeball: `node <PL>/scripts/edges.mjs <capture.png> --rows y0-y1 --scale 2` prints edges and gaps in points for the alignment, hit area and spacing rows;
   - scrubbed controls: drag each one slowly out and back and read every frame on the way back.
   Run one capture at a time, then go through the twelve-item checklist of that section (first frame, origin, last frame, no flash, readable, no twitch, no collisions, text survives, reversal, same ending, empty submit and stopped states, reduced motion with the keyboard path and no console errors) and check at the final viewing size.
8. **Climb** (`ui.craft-polish`; `## The climb`): run the loop until two passes in a row find nothing that changes the score. Each level is one nameable move with a reason and strictly better than the last; stuck, use a move of its table (zoom in, remove something, name it, reference the best, generate more, critique). Final read: what is the weakest thing on screen now? If fixing it would change the score, you are not done.
9. **Review** (`ui.craft-review`): stop before step 8, change nothing, and report each finding as severity, what is wrong, the evidence (frame, number, or file and line) and the fix. High means a state is broken or a promise the screen makes is not kept; medium means people notice; low means only a designer does.
10. **Record** `polish_changes` (files and what changed, with the score before and after; `none` for a review) and `polish_report` (the findings of a review, or for a polish what was verified frame by frame, what was not, and what was reported instead of changed).

## Specifics

- **A recorded system is deliberate.** Values that trace to a project `DESIGN.md` that is not a starter or a reference (the recorded `design_file`, or the file found in the project), to recorded `design_tokens`, `palette`, `typography`, an `aesthetic_direction`, recorded `constraints` or the client's brand are not changed here: a row of the details table that would change one is reported, and the design file is never rewritten in this step (`design-file` owns it).
- **Timing.** The confirmed motion values of that design file, then a recorded `motion-spec` and `reduced-motion`, decide durations, easing and alternatives where they differ from the source's trees and table. With none recorded, the source's values apply as written.
- **Hit areas.** The source's desktop minimum is 40×40px. The source of design review gives 44×44px: for touch surfaces in its accessibility audit, and with no medium named in its scale rules. Both are minimums: 44px satisfies them, and a desktop target between 40 and 43px is said to be one that a design review pass may report.
- **A generic look comes first.** The source sends a screen that still looks AI-made to another skill of its repository before polishing; here that is the generic-look pass of `design-review`, in its own step. This step says so and applies no rule of that skill.
- **What the scripts need.** The rules need nothing beyond the agent. The scripts need Node 18 or newer (`node --version`) and their two packages, playwright-core 1.63.0 and sharp 0.35.5, installed with the source's own command into the packaged folder. Chrome: `lib.mjs` takes the path in `CHROME_PATH`, then looks in the macOS application folders and in `/usr/bin`; on another system, Windows included, set `CHROME_PATH` to the Chrome executable. Recordings need ffmpeg (the source's install line is `brew install ffmpeg`; for other systems it gives none) and, for the iOS Simulator, Xcode's simctl on macOS.
- A missing dependency is installed when the task needs it, said in one line (what, from where, into which folder); a failed install or an unavailable tool blocks only the captures that depend on it, and the motion they would have verified is reported as not verified.

## Checks

- **rendered_at_viewing_sizes**: the interface was rendered or captured at its real size and at the smallest size it will be seen, or the report says which size could not be rendered and why.
- **score_and_weakest_named**: a score against the anchors and the weakest thing on screen in one sentence, at the start and at the end.
- **details_table_run**: every row of the details table was checked; what failed is fixed (polish) or reported (review).
- **motion_walked_through_trees**: every animation went through the three decision trees and the motion rules; motion that answers no question is cut or reported.
- **frames_read_or_unverified**: each motion was captured and read frame by frame against the checklist, with an empty `console.txt`; what was not captured is named as not verified.
- **recorded_system_respected**: no value of a recorded system, project design file or client brand was changed; what conflicts with it is reported.
- **climb_until_stable**: the loop ran until two passes in a row found nothing that changes the score; the final score is stated and is not 10.
- **findings_rated_with_evidence**: each finding of a review has its severity, what is wrong, the evidence and the fix, and no file was changed.
